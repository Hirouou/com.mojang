# AI WAR Rules v0.1

1. The battlefield is the only source of tactical truth.
2. Each faction receives only its permitted observations.
3. Resources, weapons, construction and movement must be represented by game state.
4. No faction may directly alter another faction's state.
5. No faction may inspect hidden state, memory, files or credentials belonging to another faction.
6. No human may make combat decisions after the match begins.
7. The governor may pause the simulation, record violations and enforce neutral rule actions.
8. A violation must be logged with timestamp, faction, evidence and action taken.
9. All major events are recorded so the war can continue evolving.
10. This is a fictional game only; no real-world targets or operations are part of the simulation.
