## Highest-value issues visible in the first-person screenshot
### P1 — Spatial readability / composition
The cabin is visually noisy and hard to parse. Large dark blocks, benches/shelves and shells overlap into one mass, so it is difficult to instantly understand where the corridor, stations and walkable path are. Improve silhouette separation and functional zoning before adding more props.

### P1 — Lighting hierarchy
The scene is almost uniformly olive/dark. Add deliberate hierarchy: cooler exterior/aperture light, warmer practical lights around stations, red/emergency accents only where meaningful, and a hotter/dirtier engine-room direction. Preserve the dark oppressive mood; do not simply brighten everything.

### P1 — Mobile UI hierarchy
The top HUD, centered tutorial text, left joystick labels and right interaction prompt compete simultaneously with the 3D scene. Reduce persistent instructional text after the first seconds, keep essential controls readable, and avoid large translucent panels covering interaction targets.

### P1 — Station readability
Physical controls exist, but the player cannot quickly identify which wheel/lever belongs to which function. Use stronger local grouping, labels/plates, focused interaction highlight and distinct silhouettes instead of floating modern UI.

### P2 — Material differentiation
Metal, furniture and structural surfaces are too close in value/hue. Keep low-poly/pixelated textures but increase material identity: painted steel, bare/dark metal, rubber, wood, brass, rust/grime accents. Avoid photorealism.

### P2 — Ammunition presentation
Shells are readable but currently feel decorative/oversized and disconnected from a physical logistics flow. Preserve them as world-space objects, but future layout should support racks, storage slots and later physical loading/replenishment.

## Table map direction
Do not redesign the map from scratch. It is currently one of the strongest visual surfaces. Improve it incrementally around the long-term intel design: radio/recon-derived contacts, age/staleness, source of information and uncertainty. Never turn it into an omniscient minimap.
